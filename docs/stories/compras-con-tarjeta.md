# Credit card purchases

As a household member, I want to log purchases made with a credit card so they don't reduce this month's budget but show up as what we'll pay by card next month, so that the card bill never catches us by surprise.

## Context

Today every expense counts against the calendar month of its date. A card purchase is really paid when the statement comes due, the following month, so logging it as a normal expense makes this month look tighter than it is and hides the bill that's coming. Bills already exist as Pendientes (see [cuentas-pendientes](cuentas-pendientes.md)), which count only against the month they're due in. This story adds named cards whose purchases (including cuotas) add up into one monthly bill (Resumen) per card.

A card purchase is **not** an Expense: it counts against no month's budget on its own. Its cuotas count, through the card's Resumen, against the month each Resumen is due in, and become Expenses only when that Resumen is paid.

## Acceptance criteria

### Cards

- [ ] In Ajustes, a member can create a card with a name (required, non-empty, trimmed, unique case-insensitively within the household), e.g. "Visa", "Master".
- [ ] In Ajustes, a member can rename a card with the same rules. Card names shown on Resúmenes and on purchases update; expenses already generated by a paid Resumen keep the name they were saved with.
- [ ] Cards belong to the household and are visible to all its members.

### Logging a purchase

- [ ] The add-expense form has a "Pagó con" selector: the default "Efectivo / débito" (today's behaviour, unchanged) or one of the household's cards. If the household has no cards, the selector shows only the default plus a shortcut to create a card.
- [ ] When a card is selected, the form also asks for number of cuotas (whole number, 1–24, default 1). Name, price, category, date and comments follow the same rules as a normal expense; price is the purchase's total and must be at least N × 0.01.
- [ ] Cuota amounts: total ÷ N rounded down to 2 decimals; the last cuota absorbs the remainder so the cuotas sum exactly to the total. E.g. 100 in 3 cuotas → 33.33, 33.33, 33.34.
- [ ] Cuota *k* (1-based) of a purchase dated in month M belongs to the card's Resumen for month M + *k*. Examples: a 1-cuota September purchase → October; a 3-cuota September purchase → October, November, December; a 3-cuota December purchase → January, February, March of the next year.
- [ ] Logging a card purchase leaves the remaining budget of its purchase month unchanged.
- [ ] Saving (create or edit) is rejected with "El resumen de <card> de <mes> ya está pagado." if any of its cuotas would land in an already-paid Resumen (e.g. a backdated purchase).
- [ ] The purchase appears in the movements list of its purchase month, marked with the card and cuotas (e.g. "Visa · 3 cuotas · no suma este mes") and not included in that month's totals.

### The Resumen (monthly card bill)

- [ ] For each card and each month with at least one cuota, there is one Resumen, due on day 10 of that month, shown in that month's "Por pagar" as "<card name>" with its total (the sum of its cuotas).
- [ ] While unpaid, a Resumen counts against its month's remaining budget with that total, exactly like any other pending Pendiente.
- [ ] Opening a Resumen lists its cuotas: purchase name, category, purchase date, "cuota k/N" (hidden when N = 1) and amount.
- [ ] There is no Resumen for a month with no cuotas; a Resumen whose cuotas are all removed disappears.
- [ ] Home shows "Tarjetas el mes que viene": the sum of the unpaid Resúmenes of the calendar month after today.

### Paying a Resumen

- [ ] A Resumen can't be paid before its month starts (the October Resumen is paid in October or later, never in September).
- [ ] A member marks it paid through the existing pay flow, entering the actual amount paid (defaults to the Resumen total; must be positive).
- [ ] Paying generates one expense per cuota with the purchase's name and cuota amount, in the category "Tarjeta" (created if missing) with the purchase's category as its subcategory.
- [ ] In Categorías, "Tarjeta" is its own slice (it includes the ajuste); opening it shows the breakdown by subcategory (e.g. Comida, Ropa), with the ajuste as its own "Ajuste" line. Card spending is not added to the top-level categories: "Comida" shows only non-card Comida expenses.
- [ ] Subcategories exist only for card expenses; normal expenses and Pendientes keep a single category.
- [ ] Generated expenses always count against the Resumen's month: they're dated on the payment date if paid within that month, otherwise on the last day of that month. E.g. the October Resumen paid on 12 Nov → expenses dated 31 Oct.
- [ ] If the amount paid differs from the Resumen total, the difference is recorded as one extra expense named "<card name> — ajuste" in the category "Tarjeta" (no subcategory), dated like the cuota expenses. The ajuste is negative when the amount paid is less than the total.
- [ ] Paying is all-or-nothing (Resumen status, cuota expenses and ajuste are saved together or not at all), and paying an already-paid Resumen is rejected.
- [ ] After payment the Resumen no longer counts as pending, and each cuota counts exactly once in the budget (as its generated expense). No purchase is double-counted in any month.
- [ ] Undoing a payment deletes its cuota expenses and ajuste and returns the Resumen to pending; purchases with no other paid cuota become editable again.

### Editing and deleting a purchase

- [ ] A card purchase whose cuotas are all in unpaid Resúmenes can be edited (any field, including card and cuotas) or deleted; affected Resúmenes recompute immediately.
- [ ] Once any cuota of a purchase is in a paid Resumen, the whole purchase is read-only and can't be deleted; the UI says why.

### Docs

- [ ] `CONTEXT.md` defines Tarjeta, Compra con tarjeta, Cuota, Resumen and Subcategoría, and a new ADR records that paying a Resumen creates one Expense per cuota plus an optional ajuste (unlike ADR 0004's one Expense per Pendiente).

## Out of scope

- Deleting a card.
- Closing dates per card (a purchase always lands in calendar month + 1).
- Editing a Resumen's due date.
- Currencies other than ARS / purchases in USD.
- Interest-bearing cuotas, card limits, balances or minimum payments.
- Reconciling against an imported statement.
- Paying part of a Resumen, or cancelling only the future cuotas of a purchase.
- Converting an existing normal expense into a card purchase (delete and re-log it).
